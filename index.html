Who we are

An elite academic network — built by students, for students.

Indigo Academics was founded on a compelling insight: the most effective instructor is one who recently navigated the same challenges. Our rigorously selected peer instructors are high-achieving students who have mastered advanced coursework and received specialized pedagogical training — earning verified community leadership hours in place of pay.

Every session is conducted online, combining the convenience of remote learning with the accountability of a structured, professional curriculum. Revenue is purposefully split: 30% flows directly to charitable causes, while the remainder fuels our expansion to serve more students across the country.

Session structure

Every class follows
a proven format.

Sessions run 1 hour (or 90 minutes for extended classes). Each block is precisely engineered for focused, active learning — never filler. Students leave every session with a clear picture of progress.

15–20
min
Questions & Review
Open time for the student to surface questions about previous material or current school curriculum. The instructor adapts the allocation dynamically — extended when needed, condensed when ready to advance.
20
min
Instruction Session
The instructor delivers a focused lesson on a new concept using a purpose-built slideshow — clear step-by-step exposition, worked examples, and real-time comprehension checks embedded throughout.
15–20
min
Guided Practice
The student works through carefully selected problems to apply new concepts, building procedural fluency and conceptual understanding before attempting independent work.
5–10
min
Wrap-Up & Parent Brief
The instructor assigns a targeted practice worksheet, then sends a detailed session summary to parents via email or WhatsApp — covering every concept taught, practice assigned, and the upcoming session plan.

Policies

Clear expectations.
For everyone.

We maintain transparent, professional standards for students, parents, and instructors alike. These policies are designed to protect everyone's time and ensure consistent, high-quality outcomes.

Cancellation Policy
All sessions must be cancelled or rescheduled at least 24 hours before the scheduled start time. With sufficient notice, the session may be rescheduled at the student's preference. Cancellations within the 24-hour window cannot be made up and are not eligible for a refund.
Instructor Consistency
We make every effort to maintain the same instructor for each student throughout their enrollment. When scheduling constraints require a different instructor, all members of our network are trained and certified to deliver instruction at the same standard of excellence.
Parent Communication
Instructors are required to send a detailed session summary to parents after every class via email or WhatsApp — covering concepts taught, practice assigned, and what will be covered in the next session. Transparency is not optional.
Scheduling Limits
Students may schedule up to 2 regular sessions per week. To request additional sessions beyond this limit, please contact our team directly and we will do our best to accommodate.
